What happens next

Most clients are training within a week of getting in touch.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    We meet at the gym to talk through your goals, show you around and agree the plan that suits you.

  2. 02

    Forms and payment

    We send you a link to forms covering your details, consent, terms and privacy, so your first session is spent training rather than on paperwork. You pay for your first 4 weeks by bank transfer or in cash before your first session.

  3. 03

    We book your first session

    Once your forms are in, your coach messages you to agree your regular training times.

  4. 04

    Assessment and your programme

    Your first session starts with a look at how you move and your starting strength. If you've added nutrition, we review a typical week of eating. Then you get your programme and we start training.

Good to know

Anything else? Email us and we'll answer it.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Many clients start with little or no gym experience. We teach every movement from scratch and progress at a pace that suits you.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose how many sessions a week fits your schedule and goal. Twice a week suits most people. If you want your eating to match your training, a training and nutrition plan gives you your first 4 weeks of nutrition coaching free. After that you can keep it for £120 every 4 weeks.

Which of you will I train with?

Either of us can coach you. We'll match you to the best fit for your goal, and because we share notes, you never start over if you see the other coach.

Can I do nutrition coaching without personal training?

Yes. The personalised nutrition plan is £180 every 4 weeks on its own. If you train with us, your first 4 weeks of nutrition are free, and after that it's £120 every 4 weeks on top of your training.

Will I have to follow a strict meal plan?

No. We set targets and build habits around the food you already eat, so it fits your life and lasts.

How do I stop or pause my plan?

Each 4 weeks is paid for separately, so there's nothing to cancel and no exit fee: just don't pay for the next 4 weeks. If you're away, let us know and we'll pause your plan so you don't lose any sessions.

What if I miss a session?

Give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll move it. Sessions missed with less notice are charged.

Is gym entry included?

Gym entry is not included unless stated. We'll let you know what you need before your first session.
