Good to know

Anything else? Message us before you buy and we'll answer it.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Many clients start with little or no gym experience. We teach every movement from scratch and progress at a pace that suits you.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose how many sessions a week fits your schedule and goal. Twice a week suits most people. If you want your eating to match your training, a training and nutrition plan gives you your first 4 weeks of nutrition coaching free. After that you can keep it for £150 every 4 weeks.

Which of you will I train with?

Either of us can coach you. We'll match you to the best fit for your goal, and because we share notes, you never start over if you see the other coach.

Can I do nutrition coaching without personal training?

Yes. The personalised nutrition plan is £200 every 4 weeks on its own. If you train with us, your first 4 weeks of nutrition are free, and after that it's £150 every 4 weeks on top of your training.

Will I have to follow a strict meal plan?

No. We set targets and build habits around the food you already eat, so it fits your life and lasts.

How do I stop or pause my plan?

Let us know before your next payment date and we'll stop or pause it. There's no exit fee.

What if I miss a session?

Give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll move it. Sessions missed with less notice are charged.

Is gym entry included?

Gym entry is not included unless stated. We'll let you know what you need before your first session.
